About EnergyHub

EnergyHub is an educational website that shares simple information about television energy consumption and energy efficiency in Australia.

About EnergyHub

What Is EnergyHub?

EnergyHub was created as a learning project to practise HTML, CSS and JavaScript while exploring appliance energy efficiency in Australia.

The website presents data insights about television technology, screen size, brands, star ratings and power consumption. The information is presented through simple visuals and explanations to make the data easier to understand.

Our Purpose

Why EnergyHub?

EnergyHub aims to make television energy information easier to explore and understand.
