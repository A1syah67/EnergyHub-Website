About This Site

EnergyHub is an educational website that shares information about television energy consumption and energy efficiency in Australia.

About the Website

EnergyHub was created as a learning project to practise HTML, CSS, and JavaScript while exploring appliance energy efficiency in Australia. The website presents data insights about television technology, screen size, brands, star ratings, and power consumption.
