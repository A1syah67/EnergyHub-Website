Appliance Energy Use in Australia

This site gives a simple overview of household appliance energy consumption in the Australian market.

Quick facts

  • Energy labels (star ratings) help compare appliance efficiency at a glance.
  • Standby power can add unexpected costs — turn devices fully off when not in use.
  • Choosing higher-efficiency models usually pays back through lower electricity bills.
