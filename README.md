TV Market Data Analysis

The EnergyHub website analyzes TV market data from Australia to understand TV types, sizes, brands, and power consumption.

Screen Technology
LCD (LED) – 3,923 models (~82%)
LCD – 500 models (~10%)
OLED – 321 models (~7%)

LCD (LED) is the most common type in the dataset.

Screen Sizes

The most common TV sizes are:

32" – ~770 models
24" – ~760 models
60" – ~625 models
31" – ~355 models

The dataset includes TV sizes from small screens to screens over 100".

Brands

The brands with the most TV models are:

Kogan – ~800
LG – ~720
Samsung Electronics – ~690
Samsung – ~490
Hisense – ~330
Power Consumption

The average power consumption is:

LCD – ~85W
LCD (LED) – ~120W
OLED – ~128W

LCD has the lowest average power consumption, while OLED has the highest.

Screen Size and Power

Larger TVs generally use more power.

Small TVs below 40" usually use less than 50W.
TVs between 40" and 70" usually use around 50W–250W.
Larger TVs can use around 150W–600W.
Screen Size and Star Rating

There is no clear relationship between TV size and star rating. Ratings are generally between 4 and 7 stars across different sizes.

Power Consumption by Brand

Power consumption varies between brands. Spark Electronics has a higher average power consumption of around 235W, while other brands such as Emete, Samsung Electronics, and Sony have lower averages.
